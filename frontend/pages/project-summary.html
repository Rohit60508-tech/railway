AI Constraint Optimization Engine
Google OR-Tools CP-SAT & Shadow Possession Combiner

RAKSHA PATH models the entire Indian Railways quadrilateral network as a high-dimensional mathematical constraint graph. It automatically identifies Night-Shadow (00:00–04:30) and Afternoon Lull (12:30–15:00) windows, simultaneously bundling track (Civil P-Way), traction (TRD OHE), and signaling (S&T) requisitions into synchronized multi-department possessions.

  • Zero-Conflict Path Shifting: Computes alternative time slots without holding Mail/Express trains.
  • Synergy Bundling: Saves over 4.5 hours of corridor block time daily by co-scheduling parallel teams.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Provides human-interpretable feature importance breakdowns for Section Controllers.

IRPWM 2020 Statutory Compliance
Indian Railways Permanent Way Standards

RAKSHA PATH enforces strict compliance with RDSO guidelines and the Indian Railways Permanent Way Manual (IRPWM 2020 edition):

  • Para 602 (Thermal Buckling): Restricts track disturbing maintenance when rail temperature ($t_r$) exceeds $t_d + 20^\circ\text{C}$. Integrated with OpenWeather real-time telemetry.
  • Para 268 (Track Fitness Certificate): Blocks closure without mandatory recording of gauge variation, unevenness index, cross level, and supervising SSE staff ID.
  • ACTM Vol II (25kV Catenary Safety): Mandates electrical power isolation and bonding memos before physical track possession handovers.

❓ Frequently Asked Operational Questions

What happens when a train conflict is detected during a proposed maintenance window? ▼
RAKSHA PATH automatically identifies crossing Mail/Express trains from the live COA timetable. The CP-SAT solver generates a conflict-free Alternative Window (such as shifting the slot earlier or later by 20–40 minutes) to avoid train detention. The Section Controller can click "Apply AI Alternative" to instantly sanction the shifted slot.
How does the Joint Handshake protocol work between Civil P-Way and Electrical TRD? ▼
For bundled shadow possessions involving multiple departments (e.g. track renewal with OHE wire adjustment), both SSE P-Way and SSE TRD must submit independent completion certificates. The line is not marked safe until all co-requisite departments certify track clearance and submit T-351 power reconnection memos.
Can a Section Controller manually override an AI recommendation? ▼
Yes. RAKSHA PATH operates under strict Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) governance. Officers can click "Manual Override", enter an operational justification reason (e.g. VIP Special Movement, Sudden Weather Derailment Alert), and submit. The override is immediately executed and permanently logged into the cryptographic audit trail.
